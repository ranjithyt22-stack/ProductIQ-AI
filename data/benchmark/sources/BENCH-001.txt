Product Overview:
Heavy-duty double-acting pneumatic cylinder designed for high-cycle industrial automation systems. Robust aluminum profile barrel with corrosion-resistant stainless steel piston rod.

Stroke length: 100 mm
Operating pressure: 1 to 10 bar (0.1 to 1.0 MPa)

Pneumatic port connection: G 1/4
Cushioning: Adjustable air cushioning both ends
Mounting Style: Standard ISO 15552 flange and foot mountings
Piston Rod Thread: M16x1.5

Performance:
Theoretical force at 6 bar: 1178 N (Extension)
Theoretical force at 6 bar: 990 N (Retraction)
Fluid: Compressed filtered lubricated or non-lubricated air.
