VORTEX FLUIDICS
PRODUCT SPECIFICATION - HIGH PRESSURE SOLENOID VALVE
Series: SV-HP-50
Category: Solenoid Valve

Overview:
Pilot-operated piston solenoid valve designed for reliable control of high-pressure air, water, neutral gases, and hydraulic oils in test stands and injection systems.

Technical Parameters:
Differential operating pressure: 1 to 50 bar
Nominal internal orifice: 12 mm
Flow factor (Kv): 3.2 m³/h (Water at 1 bar differential)
Port size: G 1/2 female ISO 228
Coil voltage: 230 V AC (50/60 Hz)
Power consumption: 18 VA (Inrush 36 VA)
Valve Body Material: Stainless Steel AISI 304
Seat Seal Material: PTFE / PEEK
Fluid Temperature: -20 to 120 °C
Duty Cycle: 100% ED continuous
Protection Class: IP65 according to EN 60529
