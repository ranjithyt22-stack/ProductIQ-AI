SENSOTECH INSTRUMENTS
PRODUCT DATA SHEET - INDUSTRIAL PRESSURE TRANSMITTER
Model: PT-420-16BAR
Category: Pressure Sensor

Overview:
Heavy-duty piezoresistive pressure transmitter designed for hydraulic power packs, compressed air networks, refrigeration equipment, and pump control.

Technical Data:
Measuring pressure range: 0 to 16 bar relative (gauge)
Overpressure safety limit: 32 bar
Burst pressure: 80 bar
Analog output signal: 4 to 20 mA (2-wire)
Accuracy: ±0.5 % FSO (Non-linearity, hysteresis, and repeatability)
Power supply voltage: 10 to 30 V DC
Process connection: G 1/4 B male EN 837
Wetted Parts: Stainless steel 316L
Operating Temperature: -30 to 100 °C
Electrical Connection: DIN 175301-803 Form A angled connector
Protection Degree: IP65
Response Time: < 2 ms
