APEX BEARINGS LTD.
ENGINEERING SPECIFICATION SHEET - RADIAL BALL BEARING
Designation: BB-6205-ZZ
Category: Industrial Bearing

Overview:
Single row deep groove ball bearing with pressed steel cage and non-contact metal shields (ZZ) on both sides. Pre-lubricated with high-grade lithium complex grease for maintenance-free operation.

Dimensions & Load Ratings:
Bore diameter (d): 25 mm
Outer diameter (D): 52 mm
Width (B): 15 mm
Basic dynamic load rating (Cr): 14.8 kN
Basic static load rating (Cor): 7.8 kN
Limiting speed (grease): 15000 rpm
Bearing Material: High Carbon Chromium Bearing Steel (100Cr6)
Cage Material: Pressed Sheet Steel
Operating Temperature Range: -30 to 120 °C
Tolerance Class: Normal (P0)
Radial Internal Clearance: CN (Normal)
