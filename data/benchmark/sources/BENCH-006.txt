HYDROFORCE DYNAMICS
TECHNICAL PRODUCT BULLETIN - HYDRAULIC AXIAL PISTON PUMP
Series: HP-A10V-45
Category: Hydraulic Pump

Product Overview:
Variable displacement axial piston pump in swashplate design for open circuit hydraulic industrial drives. Flow is proportional to drive speed and displacement.

Operating Parameters:
Geometric displacement: 45 cm³/rev
Nominal working pressure: 280 bar (28 MPa)
Maximum peak pressure: 350 bar (35 MPa)
Maximum drive speed: 2600 rpm
Maximum delivery flow: 117 l/min at rated speed
Drive Shaft: Splined shaft ANSI B92.1a
Mounting Flange: SAE 2-bolt mounting flange
Suction Port: SAE 1 1/2 inch 3000 psi flange
Pressure Outlet Port: SAE 1 inch 6000 psi flange
Hydraulic Fluid: Mineral oil HLP according to DIN 51524
Viscosity Range: 16 to 36 mm²/s (cSt)
